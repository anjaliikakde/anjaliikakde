# Hey there!
I’m **Anjali Kakde**, a curious mind and a passionate learner, currently pursuing my degree in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning at SISTec-R,Bhopal.

My journey into tech began with a spark—an urge to understand how machines think, learn, and solve problems. That curiosity quickly grew into a deep fascination with AI, where data meets imagination and logic turns into innovation. I found myself drawn not just to the “how,” but the “why”—why certain models work, why patterns matter, and how intelligent systems can make a real difference in the world.
I spend most of my time building models, experimenting with algorithms, and diving deep into Generative AI, especially GANs. What I love the most is that moment when abstract math turns into something visual, useful, or even beautiful. That’s where I feel most at home.

Beyond the code, I’m also a storyteller at heart. I write on Medium to make technical topics approachable and inspire others who are just stepping into the world of AI. Because to me, knowledge is best when it’s shared.
